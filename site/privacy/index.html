TripSplit Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

TripSplit is an iPhone app for small groups of friends to split trip costs and settle up. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it. TripSplit has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

What TripSplit collects

  • Your account: when you sign in with Apple, TripSplit receives an account identifier, your name, and an email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", Apple gives TripSplit a private relay address instead.
  • Your profile: the name you show friends, your Venmo username if you add one, whether you take Zelle or Apple Cash, and your notification settings.
  • Your trips: trip names, dates, and stamps; who is on each trip; expenses (description, amount, date, who paid, and how they're split); payments you log; and the activity history of those changes.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your device's push notification token, so TripSplit can tell you about activity on your trips.

TripSplit never collects or shows your phone number, never reads your contacts, and doesn't use your location. Payments themselves happen in Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Cash; TripSplit never sees or handles your money or your bank or card details.

How it's used

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your trips and balances, letting friends pay you, and sending the notifications you turned on.

Who can see it

  • People on your trips see your name, your pay methods, and the expenses, payments, and activity on the trips you share. Someone who isn't on a trip with you can't see your information.
  • Service providers: TripSplit stores data with Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in. Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications.

TripSplit doesn't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you use TripSplit. Signing out removes this phone's push token. To delete your account, email the address below. Expenses and payments you took part in stay on your friends' trips so their balances remain correct, shown under "Deleted account" instead of your name; your name, pay methods, and notification settings are erased.

Children

TripSplit isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.
